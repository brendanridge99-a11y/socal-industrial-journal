Walmart Cuts or Relocates About 1,000 Corporate Workers in Tech Consolidation

Walmart announced it would cut or relocate approximately 1,000 corporate employees in its technology and product divisions as the retail giant completes a shift to a unified global technology platform, the Wall Street Journal first reported. The restructuring was directed by Global CTO Suresh Kumar and EVP of AI Acceleration Daniel Danker, who cited the consolidation of previously siloed tech teams working on duplicate problems across Walmart U.S., Sam's Club, and international units. Affected workers are being offered internal positions or relocation to Walmart's Bentonville, Arkansas headquarters or its Northern California offices. The round brings total 2026 corporate roles affected to roughly 1,100, following an earlier 1,500-position reduction in May 2025.

Buc-ee's Accelerates Expansion With Locations In 6 More States

Texas-based travel plaza chain Buc-ee's is set to open in six new states — Arizona, Arkansas, Wisconsin, Louisiana, Kansas, and North Carolina — by the end of 2027, extending its footprint from 12 to 20 states. Arizona and Arkansas will receive their first Buc-ee's locations in 2026, with 74,000 SF stores featuring 120 fuel pumps planned for Goodyear, Arizona (June) and Benton, Arkansas (August). The chain currently operates 55 locations and plans travel centers of at least 70,000 SF in each new market. The 37th Texas location is set to open in San Marcos in July.

BisNow • May 7, 2026

EQT To Spend $1.1B Buying Into Americold Warehouses

Americold Realty Trust (NYSE: COLD) and EQT's Active Core Infrastructure fund announced a $1.3 billion joint venture covering 12 cold storage facilities across the United States, totaling approximately 124 million cubic feet of temperature-controlled capacity and over 400,000 pallet positions. EQT will acquire a 70% interest in the joint venture while Americold retains a 30% equity stake and continues as day-to-day manager; Americold expects to receive approximately $1.1 billion in net cash proceeds to repay outstanding debt. The transaction is expected to close in Q3 2026 subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals. Americold's stock jumped 17% on the announcement; the company had seen its stock fall nearly 70% since 2021 amid oversupply in the cold storage sector.
