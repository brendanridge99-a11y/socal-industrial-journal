CNBC • May 12, 2026

CPI Inflation April 2026: Prices Rose 3.8% Annually

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on May 12 that the Consumer Price Index rose 0.6% in April on a seasonally adjusted basis, pushing the annual rate to 3.8% — the highest since May 2023 and above the 3.7% consensus forecast. Core CPI, excluding food and energy, increased 0.4% for the month and 2.8% year-over-year, with the monthly rate the highest since January 2025. Energy prices jumped 3.8% for the month, accounting for more than 40% of the headline gain, with gasoline up 28.4% annually. Traders raised odds of a Fed rate hike by year-end to approximately 30%, according to CME Group data.

Federal Reserve • April 29, 2026

FOMC Holds Federal Funds Rate at 3.50%–3.75% in Divisive April Vote

The Federal Open Market Committee voted to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 3.5% to 3.75% at its April 29, 2026 meeting, marking the third consecutive meeting at which rates were held unchanged. The 8–4 vote was the most divisive FOMC decision since October 1992, with Governor Miran voting to lower rates by 25 basis points and three other members objecting to language in the statement suggesting the Fed would eventually resume cutting rates. The Committee stated it is 'strongly committed to supporting maximum employment and returning inflation to its 2 percent objective.' Kevin Warsh, President Trump's nominated successor to Chair Jerome Powell, was set for a Senate confirmation appointment on May 15.
