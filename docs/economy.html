Rapidan Energy: Hormuz Closure Through August Risks 2008-Scale Recession

Rapidan Energy Group warned that a continued closure of the Strait of Hormuz through August could trigger an economic downturn approaching the scale of the 2008 global financial crisis. The strait has been largely closed to non-Iranian shipping since the U.S. and Israel launched strikes against Iran in late February. Rapidan's base case assumes the waterway reopens in July, with Brent crude peaking near $130 per barrel and average oil demand falling by 2.6 million barrels per day over the summer. A more pessimistic scenario extending the closure through August would cause the oil supply deficit to grow to 6 billion barrels per day in the third quarter.
