Global Net Lease To Acquire Modiv Industrial REIT In $535M Deal

Global Net Lease (NYSE: GNL) announced a $535 million all-stock merger agreement to acquire Modiv Industrial (NYSE: MDV), the only publicly traded REIT exclusively focused on single-tenant industrial manufacturing real estate. Anaheim Industrial Outdoor Storage Site Secures Bridge Loan

JLL Capital Markets arranged $26 million in bridge financing for a 7.75-acre industrial outdoor storage facility at 1477 N. Jefferson St. in Anaheim, California, on behalf of borrower North Palisade Partners. The non-recourse, three-year, floating-rate loan covers a Class A property totaling 337,590 square feet at the southwest corner of North Jefferson St. and Orangethorpe Ave. The site is fully leased to a Fortune 100 logistics company that uses the facility as secured parking to support two nearby distribution centers, with 161 trailer parking stalls and two points of ingress and egress.
