West Marine Files for Chapter 11 Bankruptcy, Plans to Rationalize Its Store Footprint

West Marine, the largest U.S. boating and marine supplies retailer, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware on May 17, 2026. The company secured a Restructuring Support Agreement backed by 96.2% of its term loan lenders, 100% of its FILO lenders, and 93.9% of equity holders, with secured lenders also committing to new exit financing. West Marine's approximately 200 retail locations across 34 states and Puerto Rico will remain open during the restructuring, with the company stating it intends to 'optimize operations and rationalize its footprint.' The company's largest unsecured creditor is Garmin International at $8.57 million, followed by distribution logistics company Virtual Supply Inc. at $5.8 million. Portage Point Partners is serving as investment banker.
