West Marine Files for Chapter 11 Bankruptcy in Delaware Court

West Marine, the largest boating and marine supplies retailer in the United States, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection on May 17 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware. The pre-arranged restructuring is backed by a Restructuring Support Agreement with 96.2% of term loan lenders, 100% of FILO lenders, and 93.9% of equity holders. The company's approximately 200 retail locations across 34 states and Puerto Rico remain open, with West Marine securing consensual use of cash collateral and new exit financing commitments from secured lenders. The filing follows a leveraged buyout debt load from a 2017 acquisition and ongoing trading difficulties through 2025 and into 2026.
