Blackstone's BXDC Data Center REIT Raises $1.75B in NYSE IPO

Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust (BXDC) completed its NYSE IPO on May 14, raising $1.75 billion by selling 87.5 million shares at $20 per share, with underwriters holding a 30-day option to increase total proceeds to $2 billion. The REIT, structured as a 'blind pool' with no assets yet acquired, will target newly constructed, stabilized data centers valued between $250 million and $1.5 billion, occupied by investment-grade hyperscale tenants on long-term leases at yields of 5.75% to 7%. Blackstone has identified approximately $25 billion in potential near-term acquisitions in Northern Virginia, Ohio, Phoenix, Maryland, and Austin. Nick Pell, former president and CIO of Blackstone's Link Logistics industrial REIT, was named CEO of the new entity.

CBRE Arranges $30.9 Million Sale of Treasure Coast Commerce Center Portfolio in Stuart, Florida

CBRE arranged the $30.9 million sale of the Treasure Coast Commerce Center Portfolio, a seven-building, multitenant industrial portfolio totaling 153,198 square feet in Stuart, Florida. CBRE represented both the seller, Ted Glasrud Associates, Inc., and the buyer, NorthBridge Partners, in the transaction. No lease terms or vacancy details for the portfolio were disclosed. Small Bay Now Dominates Industrial and It Will Stay That Way

GlobeSt published an analysis on May 14, 2026 examining how small-bay industrial product has become the dominant force in the industrial market. HALO businesses and the evolution of the broader economy were cited as major demand drivers behind the shift. Small-bay and micro-warehouse assets in last-mile and infill locations continue to post vacancy rates below 5%, making them among the most competitive subsectors in the market. The analysis concludes that the structural demand for small-bay industrial is durable and unlikely to reverse.
