Global Net Lease To Acquire Modiv Industrial REIT In $535M Deal

Global Net Lease (NYSE: GNL) announced a $535 million all-stock merger agreement to acquire Modiv Industrial (NYSE: MDV), the only publicly traded REIT exclusively focused on single-tenant industrial manufacturing real estate. Modiv shareholders will receive 1.975 newly issued GNL shares per Modiv share, representing a 17% premium to Modiv's May 1, 2026 closing price of $18.82 per share. Upon closing—expected in Q3 2026 pending Modiv shareholder approval—existing GNL stockholders will own 89% of the combined company. GNL intends to repay Modiv's balance sheet debt and preferred stock using its revolving credit facility and cash on hand; the deal is expected to be immediately 4% accretive to GNL's AFFO per share.

Global Net Lease Agrees to Pay $535 Million for Modiv Industrial

Global Net Lease, a New York-based industrial-focused REIT, agreed to acquire Modiv Industrial in an all-stock transaction valued at $535 million, pending Modiv shareholder approval and expected to close by September 2026. Modiv owns 42 properties across 14 states with a majority in California, collected $39.1 million in annual base rent in 2025, and describes its portfolio as mission-critical industrial properties supporting U.S. supply chains. The national industrial vacancy rate stands at 7.6% per CoStar data and is expected to peak at 7.8% by year-end 2026. Modiv CEO Aaron Halfacre stated the company believed its portfolio was historically mispriced by the marketplace.

Southern California Landlords Hit With Biggest U.S. Industrial Leasing Drop

Industrial landlords in Southern California's Inland Empire posted one of the steepest quarterly occupancy losses of any U.S. industrial market, with tenants vacating 4 million more square feet than they moved into in Q2 2025, per CoStar data. The Inland Empire's vacancy rate reached a 15-year high of 8.5%, prompting landlords to offer rent concessions, lean into renewals, and market top-tier properties. Nationally, industrial tenants vacated more space than they moved into for the first time in 15 years, pushing the U.S. industrial vacancy rate to 7.5%, its highest level in more than a decade. IDC Logistics recently signed two major leases totaling more than 1.1 million square feet in the Inland Empire, and Dedeaux Properties secured a full-building lease in Fontana with global third-party logistics firm Pilot Air Freight.
