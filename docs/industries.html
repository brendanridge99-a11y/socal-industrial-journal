Toyota's Car Sales Slide for Third Month on Middle East Conflict

Toyota Motor Corp reported a decline in global vehicle sales for the third consecutive month, posting a year-on-year decrease of 3.7% in April to 902,015 vehicles, primarily attributed to supply chain disruptions from the ongoing Middle East conflict. The automaker reported a staggering 92% drop in exports to the Middle East, where only 2,418 vehicles were shipped. Toyota in May had already forecast a 20% decline in operating profit for the current financial year — to 3.0 trillion yen ($19 billion), well below analyst consensus of 4.59 trillion yen — citing cost and supply uncertainties from the Middle East conflict. The company said limited scope for short-term offsetting measures was contributing to the weaker earnings outlook.
