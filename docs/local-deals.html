Anaheim Industrial Outdoor Storage Site Secures Bridge Loan

JLL Capital Markets arranged $26 million in bridge financing for a 7.75-acre industrial outdoor storage facility at 1477 N. Jefferson St. in Anaheim, California, on behalf of borrower North Palisade Partners. The non-recourse, three-year, floating-rate loan covers a Class A property totaling 337,590 square feet at the southwest corner of North Jefferson St. and Orangethorpe Ave. The site is fully leased to a Fortune 100 logistics company that uses the facility as secured parking to support two nearby distribution centers, with 161 trailer parking stalls and two points of ingress and egress. The property is located 1.1 miles from State Route 91 and approximately 33 miles from both the Port of Long Beach and Port of Los Angeles. JLL's team was led by Senior Director Peter Thompson, Associate Kyle White, and Analyst Nick Englhard.

Los Angeles 'Mansion Tax' Delivers First Affordable Housing Project

CoStar reported that Los Angeles's Measure ULA transfer tax — the so-called 'mansion tax' — has delivered its first affordable housing project funded by the levy. Measure ULA applies a 4% tax on property sales above $5.3 million and 5.5% on sales above $10.6 million, and has generated approximately $1 billion across more than 1,400 transactions since taking effect in April 2023. Critics point to UCLA and RAND research estimating the policy has resulted in 1,900 fewer apartment units delivered annually, with overall construction permits in Los Angeles falling 40%. The City Council is advancing proposed amendments with a 15-year development exemption for voter consideration in November 2026.

BisNow • April 26, 2026

Inland Empire Industrial Slump Deepens, But Brokers Say Rebound May Be Near

The Inland Empire industrial vacancy rate reached 7.8% in Q1 2026, with negative net absorption of 4.7 million square feet driven by four spaces of 1 million square feet or more that went vacant in the quarter, according to CBRE. Total inventory in the market stands at 660.4 million square feet, with an 8.1% vacancy rate leaving approximately 53.6 million square feet of empty space, per Colliers. New construction deliveries dropped sharply to just 102,000 square feet in Q1 2026, down from nearly 2 million square feet in the same period a year prior. Colliers tracked average asking rents in the Inland Empire West at $1.10 per square foot and IE East at $0.90 per square foot, for a marketwide average of $1.00 per square foot.
