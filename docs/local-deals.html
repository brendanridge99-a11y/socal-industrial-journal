CoStar: Industrial Vacancy at West Coast Port Markets Exceeds 13% as Chinese Trade Volume Falls

Availability in large, newer logistics properties near major West Coast ports exceeds 13%, compared to an average of approximately 9% for East Coast port markets, per CoStar data. China's share of U.S. imports fell to just 7.5% in early 2026, down from 22% at the start of Trump's first term in 2017, directly reducing demand at Southern California port-adjacent warehouse markets. The Birtcher Logistics Center, a 492,000-square-foot building in the Inland Empire that completed construction in early 2025, remains vacant, illustrating continued challenges in the supply overhang. CoStar projects that data centers will surpass warehouses in construction spending in 2026.

CoStar • May 12, 2026 🔒

Los Angeles 'Mansion Tax' Delivers First Affordable Housing Project

CoStar reported that Los Angeles's Measure ULA transfer tax — the so-called 'mansion tax' — has delivered its first affordable housing project funded by the levy. Measure ULA applies a 4% tax on property sales above $5.3 million and 5.5% on sales above $10.6 million, and has generated approximately $1 billion across more than 1,400 transactions since taking effect in April 2023. Critics point to UCLA and RAND research estimating the policy has resulted in 1,900 fewer apartment units delivered annually, with overall construction permits in Los Angeles falling 40%. The City Council is advancing proposed amendments with a 15-year development exemption for voter consideration in November 2026.

Commercial Property Executive • April 2026

Covington Group Obtains Full Entitlements for 9.4M SF Antelope Valley Commerce Center in Palmdale, CA

Industrial real estate specialist Covington Group obtained full entitlements for Antelope Valley Commerce Center in Palmdale, California, covering up to 9.4 million square feet on 510 acres — described as one of the largest industrial entitlements ever completed in Los Angeles County. The latest entitlements cover AVCC East at just over 8 million square feet, building on the previously approved AVCC West at approximately 1.4 million square feet. Earlier in 2026, Covington also secured a $33.8 million construction loan for a 428,185-square-foot industrial development in Hesperia, California (Inland Empire) on behalf of Pixior Group, a 3PL with an option to occupy and purchase the building. The Palmdale project is located in the northern Los Angeles County industrial corridor.
