Containerized Freight Index Rises 3.62% on May 25, Up Nearly 40% Year-Over-Year

The Containerized Freight Index rose to 2,218.15 points on May 25, 2026, a gain of 3.62% from the prior day. Over the past month, the index has risen 18.29%, and is up 39.85% compared to the same period a year ago, according to trading on a contract for difference. The sharp year-over-year acceleration reflects ongoing disruptions from the closed Strait of Hormuz, elevated bunker costs, and carriers implementing blank sailings to support General Rate Increases. Drewry data separately shows transpacific rates from Shanghai to Los Angeles at $3,385 per 40-foot container, up 1% week-over-week, with carrier ONE announcing a $2,000-per-container Peak Season Surcharge effective June 1.
