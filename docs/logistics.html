Gulf States Tell Ships Not to Use Iran's Strait of Hormuz Route

Five Middle Eastern countries — Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and the United Arab Emirates — formally rejected Iran's establishment of the Persian Gulf Strait Authority to control transit through the Strait of Hormuz. In a letter to the International Maritime Organization distributed earlier this week, the five nations said commercial and merchant vessels should not engage with the PGSA or cross the waterway using Iran's designated route. The joint statement escalates the ongoing standoff over the strategically vital waterway, which has been largely closed to commercial shipping since the US-Israeli military campaign against Iran began on February 28. The letter signals a hardening of Gulf state opposition to Iran's claimed maritime authority.
