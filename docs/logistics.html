Strait of Hormuz Remains Effectively Closed; Both Middle East Maritime Corridors Blocked as Iran Conflict Continues

The Strait of Hormuz has been effectively closed since February 28, 2026, following U.S.-Israeli military strikes on Iran, with the IRGC confirming closure on March 2 and all major carriers — Maersk, CMA CGM, MSC, and Hapag-Lloyd — suspending transits. The closure blocks approximately 20% of the world's daily oil supply and 20% of global LNG trade. Simultaneously, Houthi forces resumed Red Sea attacks on February 28, eliminating near-term prospects for a return to Suez Canal routing and forcing most Asia-Europe and Asia-U.S. East Coast services to route via the Cape of Good Hope. The U.S. has simultaneously blockaded Iranian ports since April 13, while the personal saving rate has fallen to 3.6%, its lowest since 2022, amid surging energy-driven inflation.
