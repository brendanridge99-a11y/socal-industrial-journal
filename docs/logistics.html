Drewry Supply Chain Advisors· June 11, 2026

Transpacific Container Rates Surge as Early 2026 Peak Season Drives Shanghai–LA Spot Rates to $4,683 Per FEU; Carriers Add Peak Season Surcharges

The Drewry World Container Index rose 3% to $3,549 per 40-foot container for the week ending June 11, driven by rate increases on Transpacific and Asia–Europe trade routes. Shanghai-to-Los Angeles spot rates climbed 3% to $4,683 per FEU, while Shanghai-to-New York rates rose 7% to $5,870 per FEU. Drewry confirmed from multiple sources that the 2026 peak season began earlier than usual, with demand supported by shippers accelerating imports ahead of potential U.S. tariff changes expected in July and additional cargo demand tied to the 2026 FIFA World Cup. MSC announced new base rates for Asia–North Europe of $6,000 per FEU effective June 15, while CMA CGM and ONE announced Peak Season Surcharges of $500–$600 per 20-foot container effective the same date.

Strait of Hormuz Closure Approaches 100 Days; U.S. Military Shoots Down Two Iranian Drones on June 12 Amid Peace Deal Negotiations

The U.S. military shot down two Iranian attack drones near the Strait of Hormuz early on June 12 after Iranian forces reportedly fired on a transiting commercial vessel, a senior U.S. defense official confirmed. The Strait of Hormuz has been effectively closed to commercial shipping since late February 2026, when Iran imposed a blockade following the start of the U.S.-Iran conflict; major container shipping companies including Maersk, CMA CGM, and Hapag-Lloyd have suspended transits through the strait.
