China Restricts Chemical Exports to US in Fentanyl Crackdown

China imposed new export controls on key chemical ingredients shipped to the US, Mexico, and Canada, framing the move as cooperation with Washington on curbing drug trafficking. The targeted substances are primary building blocks used to manufacture illicit fentanyl. The restrictions signal continued evolution in US-China trade and regulatory coordination following the broader tariff truce.
