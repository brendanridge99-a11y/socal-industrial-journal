CARB Votes on Major Cap-and-Invest Program Amendments That Would Reshape California's Carbon Market Through 2045

The California Air Resources Board is scheduled to vote today, May 28, 2026, on proposed amendments to the state's Cap-and-Invest program, with proceedings potentially extending into May 29. The April proposal would add up to 118 million Market Disruption Insurance (MDI) allowances above the emissions cap, increase industry free allowances, reduce electric utility allowances, and put offsets under the cap. Critics including the Legislative Analyst's Office and 45 environmental groups warn the changes could reduce Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund revenues to approximately $2 billion per year and undermine California's 2030 emissions targets. The most recent quarterly Cap-and-Invest auction, held the day prior, sold all 49,634,209 current vintage allowances at a settlement price of $28.81, generating an estimated $770 million for the GGRF — well below the $1.8 billion that would have been raised at CARB's own projected price of $68.
