U.S. to Award $2 Billion to Quantum Computing Firms, Take Equity Stakes

The Wall Street Journal reported, citing the Commerce Department, that the Trump administration is awarding $2 billion in grants to nine quantum-computing companies in deals that include U.S. government equity stakes. IBM is the largest recipient at $1 billion and will use the CHIPS Act funding to establish Anderon, described as America's first dedicated quantum chip manufacturing facility in Albany, New York. GlobalFoundries is set to receive $375 million, while D-Wave Quantum, Rigetti Computing, and Infleqtion are each expected to receive approximately $100 million. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick confirmed the deals; shares of quantum companies surged 7% to 30% on the news.

Walmart Posts Strong Q1 FY27 Results but Distribution Fuel Costs Weigh on Operating Income

Walmart reported Q1 FY27 results on May 21, 2026, with U.S. comparable sales growing 4.1% and global e-commerce up 26%, but operating income was negatively affected by 250 basis points from higher fuel costs in distribution and fulfillment. Drewry Shipping Consultants · May 21, 2026 via Logistics

Drewry World Container Index — May 21: Transpacific Rates Rise, ONE Files $2,000 PSS for June 1

Drewry's May 21 World Container Index shows freight rates from Shanghai to Los Angeles increased 1% to $3,385 per 40ft container, while Shanghai to New York grew 2% to $4,317 per 40ft container. Ocean Network Express (ONE) has announced a Peak Season Surcharge of $2,000 per 40ft container on Transpacific eastbound cargo, effective June 1, with CMA CGM announcing new FAK levels above current rates for Asia-Europe effective the same date. Seven blank sailings have been announced on the Transpacific for the coming week, tightening capacity. Drewry expects rates to increase further in coming weeks as early peak season trends emerge across East-West trade lanes, compounded by ongoing Middle East geopolitical tensions and elevated bunker costs.
