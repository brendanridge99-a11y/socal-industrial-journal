Walmart Q1 Sales Hit $175.7B as E-Commerce Surges 26%

Walmart reported first-quarter FY27 net sales of $175.7 billion, exceeding the $174.8 billion analyst consensus, with adjusted EPS of $0.66 and net income of $5.33 billion. U.S. comparable sales grew 4.1%, while global e-commerce sales rose 26%, driven by faster delivery speeds and expansion of its third-party marketplace. The company left full-year FY27 guidance unchanged but warned that high fuel costs tied to Strait of Hormuz disruptions would pressure store prices and consumer spending in Q2. Walmart shares fell approximately 7% following the cautious outlook.

Industrial Realty Group Takes Portfolio Public

Los Angeles-based Industrial Realty Group is combining 98 industrial properties into a new publicly traded REIT through a definitive contribution agreement with Connecticut-based mortgage lender Sachem Capital Corp., creating IRG Realty Trust with an implied enterprise value of approximately $3.4 billion. The combined entity is expected to rank among the top 10 publicly listed industrial REITs by enterprise value, with IRG owning approximately 94.1% of the new company via operating partnership units and existing Sachem shareholders retaining roughly 5.9%. The 98 properties — described as mission-critical industrial infrastructure leased to manufacturing, distribution, and logistics tenants — carry a gross real estate asset value of $2.9 billion; Sachem's approximately $470 million in existing mortgage loan assets will also be included. The transaction, unanimously approved by Sachem's board, is expected to close by end of 2026 subject to Sachem shareholder approval, with Scotiabank advising IRG and Piper Sandler advising Sachem.
