Hughes Marino· May 26, 2026

The Worst Commercial Real Estate Market in 30 Years: What Office & Industrial Tenants Must Know in 2026

Hughes Marino co-founder David Marino assessed the current commercial real estate environment as the worst in more than 30 years, worse than both the post-dot-com correction and the 2008 financial crisis. Marino stated the industrial market is by several measures worse than the office market, noting approximately 250 million square feet of industrial sublease space on the market nationally versus 170 million square feet of office sublease. The Inland Empire was cited as the most acute correction, with the industrial availability rate now two to three times above pre-COVID levels; rents remain around $1.00–$1.10 per square foot despite elevated availability. Marino argued that landlord capital structures and a broker community predominantly representing landlords are artificially propping up face rates through concessions not reflected in published figures.
