Port of Long Beach Sets 2025 Record at 9.9M TEUs, Plans $3.2B in Infrastructure Ahead of 2026

The Port of Long Beach concluded 2025 handling a record 9.9 million TEUs — its busiest year in its 115-year history — securing a 48.9% cargo share among San Pedro Bay ports. Newly appointed CEO Dr. Noel Hacegaba projected another 9 million TEU year in 2026, targeting a top-five year by cargo volume. The port announced $3.2 billion in infrastructure investments to double capacity by 2050, including the $1.8 billion Pier B On-Dock Rail Support Facility scheduled for completion in 2032. Hacegaba noted tariffs have reshaped trade patterns, with China's share of cargo declining from 70% to 60% over six years as Southeast Asian countries gained share.
