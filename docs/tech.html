AI Chipmaker Cerebras Shares Soar 89% After Year's Biggest IPO

AI chipmaker and data center firm Cerebras Systems raised $5.55 billion in its Nasdaq IPO, priced at $185 per share — above the marketed range — giving it an initial market value of approximately $40 billion. BisNow • May 13, 2026

Proprietary Data Obsession Is Holding Real Estate Back From AI

At Bisnow's New York AI & Technology event held at Silverstein Properties' 7 World Trade Center on May 13, industry executives discussed how real estate firms' reluctance to share proprietary data with AI platforms is limiting adoption of the technology. Fundrise CEO Ben Miller stated that real estate companies are 'afraid to adopt the cutting-edge' AI tools due to concerns about data exposure to large language models. Onyxia Cyber CEO Sivan Tehila warned of 'shadow AI,' where employees use AI tools without authorization, potentially leaking sensitive company data. IBM research cited at the event found that over one-third of employees admit sharing sensitive work information with AI tools without employer permission.

AI Expected to Boost Demand for US Industrial, Office, Retail Space by 12% in Next Decade

Artificial intelligence adoption will boost U.S. property demand for industrial, office, and retail space by approximately 12% over the next decade, adding about 330 million square feet, according to Cushman & Wakefield's baseline scenario forecast reported by CoStar. The report counters widespread fears that AI would reduce the need for commercial real estate, predicting instead that productivity gains from AI will generate net new demand. The forecast represents Cushman & Wakefield's baseline scenario, with upside scenarios projecting even greater demand growth.
